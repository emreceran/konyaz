Privacy

Personal data protection and privacy

This page explains what the information you send us through the forms on our website is used for, who it is shared with and what rights you have over it.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

1. Data controller

This privacy notice has been prepared by Konyaz as data controller under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”).

Address: İçerenköy, Eryılmazlar Sk. No:3 D:11, 34752 Ataşehir / İstanbul
Email: info@vohdigital.com

2. What personal data do we process?

  • Identity data: first and last name
  • Contact data: email address, phone number
  • Professional data: company name, industry, number of employees
  • Request data: your message, the template you download; if you ask for an evaluation, the figures you entered into the tools and the result
  • Transaction security data: IP address, browser information, form submission time, result of the bot check

The figures you enter into the tools are calculated in your browser. Unless you send them through a form, they do not reach us and are not stored.

3. For what purposes do we process it?

  • to answer your question, message or request for a quote,
  • to fulfil your template download or result evaluation request and to review your result together with you,
  • to contact you about our services and to let you know about new tools and templates,
  • to send business development e-mails to companies we find in public sources (details in section 10),
  • to protect the forms against automated submissions and misuse, and to keep the website secure,
  • to measure how the website is used without identifying individuals.

4. Method of collection and legal basis

Your personal data is collected electronically when you fill in the contact form, the template download form or the “Evaluate my result” form on the website.

  • Answering your request and preparing a quote: processing is directly related to the conclusion of a contract (Article 5(2)(c) KVKK)
  • Form security and website measurement: our legitimate interest, provided that it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms (Article 5(2)(f) KVKK)
  • Promotional communication and transfer of data abroad: the explicit consent you give with the checkbox on the form (Article 5(1) and Article 9 KVKK)

5. To whom do we transfer it and why?

Your data is not sold and is not shared with third parties for promotional purposes. It is shared only to the extent necessary with the following service providers, which we use to run the website and the forms:

  • Netlify, Inc. (USA): website hosting; receiving and storing form submissions and forwarding them to us by email
  • Google LLC (USA): bot check (reCAPTCHA) and the email service that receives form notifications
  • Cloud Clusters (USA): hosting of the server owned by Konyaz on which form details are kept as customer relationship management (CRM) records
  • Umami (website analytics service): visit statistics without cookies and without identifying individuals

Since these providers’ servers are located outside Türkiye, your data is transferred abroad. The transfer is based on your explicit consent under Article 9 KVKK. Where required by law, data may also be shared with authorised public authorities and institutions.

6. How long do we keep it?

Form data is kept for no more than 2 years from our last contact with you. If a contract is concluded between us, the periods set out in the applicable legislation apply. When the period expires, or at your request, the data is erased, destroyed or anonymised.

7. Cookies and browser storage

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies; visits are measured without cookies. On pages with a form, Google reCAPTCHA may use its own cookies for the bot check.

When you submit a form, your name, company and contact details are kept only in your own browser (localStorage) so that you are not asked for them again on your next download. You can remove them by clearing your browser’s site data.

8. Your rights

Under Article 11 KVKK, by applying to Konyaz you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is being processed,
  • request information about it if it has been processed,
  • learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose,
  • know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it has been transferred,
  • request its correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • request its erasure or destruction under the conditions set out in Article 7 KVKK,
  • request that the correction, erasure or destruction be notified to the third parties to whom the data has been transferred,
  • object to a result to your detriment arising solely from the analysis of the data by automated systems,
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.

You may withdraw your explicit consent at any time.

9. How to apply

You can send your requests by email to info@vohdigital.com or in writing to the address above. In your application, clearly state your name, contact details where we can reach you and your request. Applications are concluded free of charge within 30 days at the latest, depending on their nature.

10. Business development e-mails

Your company may have received an e-mail from Konyaz even if you have never used our website. We send these e-mails only to companies, on a business matter, and to the business contact details the company itself has published.

  • Data processed: company name, sector and city; the corporate contact address published by the company (such as info@ or sales@); the source we took this information from. We do not write to addresses belonging to a specific person.
  • Source: public corporate sources such as the company’s own website, business council and association member pages, and trade fair exhibitor lists. We do not write to guessed or personal addresses.
  • Purpose: to briefly introduce our services to your company and offer a free digitalisation report. We send at most 3 e-mails on one topic.
  • Legal basis: corporate contact addresses do not identify a specific person. The name and contact details of a person who replies to our e-mail are processed to answer their request and prepare an offer (KVKK art. 5/2-c). Messages sent to companies in Türkiye are registered in the Message Management System (İYS) under Law No. 6563.
  • Transfer: e-mails are sent through the Google LLC (USA) e-mail service and the Instantly (USA) sending tool; if you reply, your details are kept as a CRM record on the Cloud Clusters (USA) server.
  • Retention: details of companies that do not reply are deleted after 12 months at the latest.

If you would like us not to write again, simply reply “no”; your company is removed from the list within 3 business days at the latest, and only your address is kept on a do-not-contact list so that we do not write again. You can ask for all your data to be deleted at info@vohdigital.com.
