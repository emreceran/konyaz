Custom Odoo Module Development

Standard Odoo covers most of your business; for the remaining part that is specific to you, we develop custom modules. Without moving to Enterprise, without paying for licences — and the code is yours.

Let's Talk About Your Project What's Included?

Ready-made Odoo modules cover 80% of your business; but the 20% that makes the difference is specific to you. Your own calculation logic, a special approval flow, a report particular to your industry or an integration with another system — none of these comes with a standard installation.

We close this gap with custom modules developed on Odoo Community. Without being forced into an Enterprise licence and without touching the core, we write clean modules that do not break with updates. The resulting code and system are entirely yours.

FAQ

About custom Odoo modules

Can custom modules be developed on Odoo Community?

Yes. Community is fully open source and is the edition best suited to custom module development. We meet your needs without moving to Enterprise.

Do I have to buy an Enterprise licence?

No. Most of your needs are met with Community plus custom modules. We consider Enterprise only if it is really necessary.

Will the module break when Odoo is updated?

We develop it as a separate module without touching the core, so it carries over through version upgrades with the fewest possible problems.

Will the source code belong to us?

Yes. The source code of the modules we develop, and the system, are entirely yours.
