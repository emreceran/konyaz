Barcode-based · Odoo-integrated inventory

Barcode Inventory and Warehouse Management

We digitise your warehouse with barcodes: goods receipt, transfers, stock counts and stock lookup are done on a phone, and every movement is posted to Odoo immediately.

Let's Talk About Your Project What's Included?

Manual stock tracking produces errors even in the most careful team. Wrong counts, lost items, uncertainty over "is it in stock?" and unnecessary orders — all of them are the price of keeping stock on paper or in Excel.

With our barcode inventory app every movement is entered by scanning, instantly and accurately. Warehouse and stock data is in Odoo in real time; nobody works from guesswork.

Capabilities

What does it do in the warehouse?

Every warehouse movement in your pocket, from goods receipt to stock count.

Goods receipt

Receive incoming items by barcode and match them to the purchase order.

Transfers & shipping

Transfers between warehouses and shelves, and shipment preparation.

Stock count

Fast, error-free stock counts by barcode.

Min. stock alerts

Automatic alerts for items that fall to a critical level.

Lot / serial tracking

Traceability by lot and serial number.

Real-time sync

Every movement is posted to Odoo in real time.

FAQ

About the inventory app

Do we need handheld scanners or phones?

Either works. We develop it to run on Android handheld terminals or on ordinary phones and tablets.

Does it work without Odoo?

You get the best results with Odoo; if you do not have Odoo, we implement that too.

Is there lot and serial number tracking?

Yes, we can set up traceability by lot and by serial number.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, we design it to work offline; it synchronises when the connection returns.
